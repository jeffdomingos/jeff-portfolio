        ## Contexto e Descoberta
        
        Em meados de 2022 a companhia liderava o mercado com expectativa de hipercrescimento. Minha missão inicial no squad de Commerce era otimizar a conversão do e-commerce B2C (`loja.voltzmotors.com`) e evoluir o Portal do Parceiro, a plataforma de back-office B2B utilizada pelas equipes internas e pela rede de lojas físicas para gerenciar pedidos e inventário.
        
        Contudo, ao mergulhar nos processos internos, concluí que acelerar vendas sem ajustar a infraestrutura operacional apenas agravaria a saúde financeira do negócio.
        
        Iniciei um diagnóstico pragmático para investigar a causa raiz do problema, triangulando dados de três frentes:
        1. Implementei pop-ups no site para recrutamento de usuários e conduzi entrevistas qualitativas via Hotjar.
        2. Auditei os chats de atendimento e as interações de vendas no CRM comercial (Bitrix24).
        3. Mapeei os relatórios de atendimento e o tempo de resolução de chamados internos no Zendesk.
        
        As evidências foram conclusivas: o discurso comercial prometia prazos irreais, gerando frustração no pós-venda. Nos bastidores, parte relevante da capacidade do time de engenharia estava comprometida executando scripts manuais no banco de dados para alterar o status dos pedidos e permitir que os clientes pudessem pagar o saldo restante.

        ## Context and Discovery
        
        In mid-2022, the company led the market with hyper-growth expectations. My initial mission in the Commerce squad was to optimize the conversion of the B2C e-commerce (`loja.voltzmotors.com`) and evolve the Partner Portal, the B2B back-office platform used by internal teams and the physical store network to manage orders and inventory.
        
        However, when diving into internal processes, I concluded that accelerating sales without adjusting the operational infrastructure would only worsen the business's financial health.
        
        I initiated a pragmatic diagnosis to investigate the root cause of the problem, triangulating data from three fronts:
        1. I implemented pop-ups on the site for user recruitment and conducted qualitative interviews via Hotjar.
        2. I audited customer service chats and sales interactions in the commercial CRM (Bitrix24).
        3. I mapped the service reports and internal ticket resolution times in Zendesk.
        
        The evidence was conclusive: the commercial speech promised unrealistic deadlines, generating frustration post-sale. Behind the scenes, a relevant part of the engineering team's capacity was compromised executing manual scripts in the database to change order statuses and allow customers to pay their remaining balance.

        ## Strategic Execution and Collaboration
        
        The work was orchestrated by the squad's leadership triad, acting in direct partnership between me (Design Lead), Product leadership (PM), and technical leadership (Tech Lead).
        
        ### 1. Intentional Friction and Transparency at Checkout (B2C)
        
        The previous model required only a R$ 500.00 down payment for reservation but omitted logistical complexity. To ensure financial breathing room and filter actual purchase intent, the board established the initial down payment increase to R$ 4,222.00. My responsibility was to translate this decision into a transparent journey.
        
        There was strong fear from commercial areas that highlighting the real 24-week deadline would drop conversion. I supported the need for change by translating qualitative dissatisfaction data into financial risk. I demonstrated that hiding the deadline generated a cash illusion: revenue entered in the short term, but evaporated weeks later with chargebacks, operational fees, and lawsuits.
        
        With this, we proved that it was more sustainable to focus on the retention of qualified customers. I designed the new flow with intentional friction, ensuring full user awareness about the parts import, sea freight, and national assembly stages before payment confirmation.
        
        * **Previous Flow:** Delivery time positioned secondary on page scroll, with little clarity about the manufacturing stages.
        * **New Flow:** Structured visual card highlighting the 24-week estimate and detailing the three supply chain phases.

        ### 2. Prioritized Module: Structuring the Back-Office (B2B)
        
        To solve the inefficiency behind the scenes, I investigated the logistics flow in the Partner Portal and identified the bottleneck holding up the operation. The internal team took up to two days crossing data in spreadsheets to assemble delivery batches. In the end, they needed to open a Zendesk ticket for developers to manually change the order status in the database, releasing the final balance payment option for the customer. This process added up to 48 hours of wait time.
        
        **The MVP Strategic Decision:** The ideal scenario required complex automation and direct integration with the factory, an unfeasible scope for the moment. To support the creation of a lean V1 (the Prioritized menu), I gathered and crossed three decisive operational metrics:
        
        * **Service Delay:** I identified in Zendesk that the window between the logistics request and the technical execution took up to 48 hours.
        * **Retained Capital:** I crossed the factory's assembly capacity (about 90 motorcycles per week) with the average value of the outstanding balance (R$ 15,000.00). I calculated that batches equivalent to more than R$ 1.3 million were retained weekly awaiting a simple status change.
        * **Engineering Cost:** In alignment with the Tech Lead, we mapped that context switching to run manual scripts consumed about 30 monthly hours from developers.
        
        With this data, I defined that the MVP would focus exclusively on the final node of the process. I designed an interface in the Partner Portal where the operations team imported the list of batch order IDs via structured file and automatically triggered the status update. We absorbed the previous spreadsheet triage to ensure immediate billing of the available motorcycles.
