        ## Overview & AI Directive: Pioneering AI Workflows

        During my tenure as Senior Product Designer for B2B Solutions at Afya (Brazil’s leading medical education and healthcare software platform), corporate leadership issued an executive directive to prioritize AI-powered solutions.

        At the time, AI tool integration was brand new to our design, product, and engineering teams. I took on a pioneering role within the design organization, leading hands-on experimentation with emerging AI tools to establish a viable workflow for rapid ideation, user testing, and developer handoff.

        Within the RX PRO enterprise platform (connecting pharmaceutical companies directly with physicians), we identified a critical business gap: a significant segment of physicians received physical sample boxes but received zero in-person visits from sales representatives. To solve this, we designed an AI Digital Representative to provide continuous, evidence-backed "Virtual Coverage."

        ## Product Metrics Framework for Conversational AI

        Working alongside product management, a key UX challenge was defining what success meant for an AI agent integrated into WhatsApp and Web interfaces. Moving beyond low-level infrastructure logs, I helped structure a four-tier Product UX Metrics Framework:

        1. **Activation Metrics:** Tracking Read Rate, Response Rate, and Opt-out rates following sample delivery.
        2. **Qualified Engagement (Primary KPI):** Defined specifically as a session where a physician completes at least 3 conversation turns OR explicitly requests a clinical deep dive (such as posology, drug interactions, or trial data).
        3. **AI/UX Quality:** Monitoring Fallback Rates, Handoff Rates (escalation to human specialists), and sentiment analysis.
        4. **Business & Virtual Coverage:** Measuring declared prescribing intent, scientific content consumption, and repeat channel usage among unvisited physicians.

        ### Post-Processing Log Analytics Architecture
        To avoid complex real-time orchestration middleware during early pilots, we designed a lightweight analytics pipeline: exporting raw WhatsApp conversation logs, running them through an LLM prompt parser, and outputting structured JSON data (tracking sentiment, fallback count, and intent) directly into product dashboards.

        ## Prototyping Evolution: From Figma Make to Production Code

        Because AI tools were rapidly evolving, I deliberately tested multiple prototyping environments to evaluate how design assets, interactive logic, and code generation could best serve our team.

        ### Phase 1: Figma Make & Design System Integration
        I started by experimenting with **Figma Make** directly inside our team's standard design environment. This allowed us to test how AI-generated layout concepts could connect with our existing Design System tokens, evaluate asset sharing dynamics, and maintain a centralized single source of truth for design assets.

        ### Phase 2: NLU-First Interactive Prototype (Lovable)
        To test dynamic conversational flows without building a full backend, I migrated to **Lovable** to construct an interactive React/TypeScript web prototype mirroring the RX PRO UI. The prototype validated 5 key conversational states:
        * **Screen 1 (NLU Immediate):** Sample delivery confirmation with open text input (no forced buttons).
        * **Screen 2 (Post-Confirmation):** Dynamic display of 7 interactive clinical suggestion pills (posology, adverse effects, trial data).
        * **Screen 3 (RAG Interaction):** Natural language processing handling complex queries (e.g., dosage calculation for a 70kg patient) with inline document citations (`Bula_Oficial_Atentah.pdf`).
        * **Screen 4 (Human Handoff):** Triaged escalation flow (Scientific Query, Logistics Issue, Other).
        * **Screen 5 (Unhappy Path):** Empathetic non-delivery handling ("Didn't receive sample") with automated address verification sub-flows.

        ## Healthcare RAG Refinement & Regulatory QA

        Designing conversational AI for regulated pharmaceutical products requires zero tolerance for medical hallucination. In collaboration with QA and engineering leads, we conducted 23+ evaluation scenarios to harden the RAG pipeline:

        * **PDF Document Sanitization:** Identified that original monograph PDFs contained heavy image layers causing text extraction failures in the vector store. The engineering team converted source documents into clean text structures prior to PDF re-generation, dramatically improving retrieval accuracy.
        * **Posology Hallucination Prevention:** Corrected model errors where acute diarrhea and irritable bowel syndrome dosages were merged under a single recommendation. Prompt engineering was adjusted to strictly segregate clinical conditions.
        * **Identity Authentication Lock:** Enforced a mandatory conditional gate requiring the agent to confirm the user's physician identity before disclosing sensitive product data.
        * **Token Limit & Error Handling:** Managed 1024 token input limits by building user-facing graceful error states for long clinical case submissions.

          > Building modular, code-backed prototypes and clear technical documentation ensures that discovery work holds long-term value. When design and engineering are deeply aligned from day one, the foundation remains strong enough to survive leadership transitions and eventually deliver real business impact.
