        ## Overview & AI Directive

        During my tenure as a Senior Product Designer for B2B Solutions at Afya (Brazil’s leading medical education and healthcare software platform), corporate leadership issued an executive directive to prioritize AI-powered solutions.

        The core challenge was navigating the exploration of emerging AI opportunities while maintaining velocity on core operational B2B SaaS features. My response was to integrate AI on two distinct levels:
        
        1. **Methodological Integration:** Leveraging AI tools to dramatically accelerate design ideation, technical validation, and code-level prototyping.
        2. **Product Feature Integration:** Designing AI-driven conversational assistants, RAG architectures, and intelligent workflow automation directly into Afya's enterprise healthcare ecosystem.

        ## AI Opportunity Exploration: 5 Strategic Paths

        To map where AI could deliver the highest ROI across our B2B ecosystem (linking pharmaceutical companies, sales representatives, and physicians), I led the discovery and UX framework for 5 strategic initiative paths:

        1. **AI Rep Training via Interactive Role-Play:** Simulating diverse physician personas (skeptical, receptive, critical) to provide real-time sales pitch and compliance feedback.
        2. **Real-Visit Transcript Analysis:** AI evaluation of physical visit audio transcripts against Medical/Legal/Regulatory (MLR) brand guidelines.
        3. **24/7 Medical Science Assistant (Farma Assist):** An instant, RAG-grounded conversational interface for physicians to answer complex clinical questions, dosage, and sample delivery status.
        4. **Gamified Microlearning for Reps:** Daily AI-generated micro-training quizzes delivered directly in the rep's workflow.
        5. **Intent-Driven Contextual Cards:** AI triggers matching physician search patterns and CME courses with evidence-based product cards.

        ## AI-Assisted Rapid Prototyping Methodology

        To reduce friction between concept, user feedback, and technical feasibility (especially when strategic product direction was evolving), I introduced and heavily experimented with an AI-assisted rapid prototyping stack.

        ### Tools & Workflows Utilized

        * **Figma Make:** Accelerated UI component exploration, design system asset generation, and rapid layout iteration.
        * **Lovable:** Enabled instant full-stack web application prototyping to test dynamic interaction flows and conversational logic.
        * **VS Code + GitHub Copilot:** Facilitated frontend code generation, allowing me to build functional React/TypeScript interfaces for realistic user testing.
        * **Antigravity:** Used for AI-assisted architecture thinking, algorithmic logic design, and rapid technical problem-solving.

        ### Impact of this Methodology

        * **Accelerated Time-to-Prototype:** Reduced exploration cycles by ~70% (from 3-4 weeks down to 3-5 days per functional prototype), delivering code-backed prototypes for stakeholder review.
        * **Elevated Technical Comfort:** Expanded the product team's understanding of AI capability boundaries and frontend execution details.
        * **Bridge Between Design & Code:** Expanded my personal skillset beyond traditional UI/UX into code generation, technical architecture, and developer collaboration.
        * **Standardized Exploration:** Established AI-assisted rapid prototyping as a recognized, viable framework for future product discovery across the company.

        ## Key Case: AI-Powered Digital Pharmaceutical Representative (Apsen MVP)

        ### The Hypothesis & Product Goals
        
        Within the RX PRO enterprise platform (connecting pharmaceutical companies with physicians), we explored whether an intelligent conversational AI agent could augment the work of human pharmaceutical representatives by:

        * **Product Training & Enablement:** Instantly answering complex queries regarding drug indications, dosages, and clinical trials.
        * **Physician Engagement:** Serving as a first-level conversational interface for doctors seeking instant information outside business hours.
        * **Workflow Friction Reduction:** Automating sample requests and drug documentation distribution directly within the platform.

        ### Execution, RAG Architecture & MLR Guardrails

        Using my AI-assisted prototyping stack alongside engineering, I took hands-on ownership of the UX design, technical grounding, and safety constraints:

        * **LangChain & RAG Vector Backend:** Collaborated with developers on a **LangChain** backend architecture fetching verified data from clinical trial documents and official product monographs (`Monografia Floracol.pdf`).
        * **23 QA & Grounding Test Scenarios:** Designed and validated 23 rigorous QA evaluation scenarios testing clinical evidence accuracy (e.g. Erdoğan 2012, Tremblay 2023, Baştürk 2016, İşlek 2015 clinical trials) with inline source citation badges (`[1 fonte]`).
        * **Anti-Hallucination & Out-of-Scope Constraints:** Designed strict conversational guardrails that politely correct false prompt assumptions without agreeing with errors, and automatically decline off-label / out-of-scope medical requests.
        * **Functional Prototype Delivery:** Delivered a working React chatbot prototype simulating real-time sample follow-ups and scientific comparisons.

        ### Outcome & Strategic Learnings

        * **The Result:** We delivered a functional, interactive web chatbot prototype that demonstrated real-time conversational capabilities, evidence-backed responses, and source citation.
        * **The Reality:** Despite technical success, the project lacked a validated commercial model and clear B2B value proposition. It was initially placed on hold pending strategic realignment.
        * **Post-Departure Update:** I later learned from former colleagues that the prototype ultimately served as the foundation for the final product, which was successfully launched and well received, validating our initial UX and technical exploration.
        * **The Key Lesson: *"AI is an Amplifier, Not a Savior"***:
          > AI-assisted prototyping is extraordinary for accelerating validation and turning complex ideas into functional code. However, an AI agent cannot compensate for an unvalidated business model. True design maturity lies in recognizing technical success while holding product strategy accountable to real customer value.
